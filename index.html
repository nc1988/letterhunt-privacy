Letter Hunt – Privacy Policy

Developer: tpham · Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short: Letter Hunt is a word game made for children. The game does not ask for, collect or share any personal information. Progress stays on your device. The Android version shows child-safe ads from Google AdMob, which can be removed with a one-time purchase through Google Play.

1. Who we are

Letter Hunt ("the game") is made by an independent developer, tpham. If you have any question about this policy or your child's privacy, write to phamdotruong@gmail.com.

2. Information the game does not collect

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to share pictures or messages. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, location or contacts, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves on your device:

  • game progress (words found, games played, tutorial seen);
  • settings (sound on or off);
  • word lessons you type into the "Custom game" screen;
  • whether "Remove ads" has been bought.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own Google account backup.

4. Ads (Android version)

The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob: a short video you can choose to watch to get a hint, and an occasional full-screen ad after a finished game. Because the game is made for children, every ad request is set up as:

  • child-directed and for users under the age of consent, so ads are not personalized and not based on interests or past behavior;
  • limited to ads rated suitable for general audiences (G);
  • without the Android advertising ID (the game does not request it).

To show and measure ads, Google may still process limited technical information such as the device type, operating system version, IP address and approximate location derived from it. Google handles this under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases (Android version)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the game never sees card or payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether "Remove ads" is owned on this Google account. Before the purchase screen opens, a "grown-ups only" question makes sure an adult is present.

6. Web version (itch.io)

The browser version has no ads and no purchases. Progress is saved in your browser's local storage on your computer only. The site hosting the game (itch.io) has its own privacy policy.

7. Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR rules for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe information about your child has been collected, contact us at the address above and we will help.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
